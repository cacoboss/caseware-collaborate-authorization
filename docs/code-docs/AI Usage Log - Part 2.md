## Notes for the four questions

Raw material only. These get written up once the slice is finished.

- **IA1** — entries 5, 8, 10, 11, 15, 16, 22. Every one was found by turning the design into something executable, never by re-reading the design. Two of them (10, 11) were caught by a failing test, and neither was visible in the design document.
- **IA2** — entries 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 13, 18, 20, 21. Entry 13 is the one to lead with: the AI's answer was reasonable and would have left a namespace cycle hidden behind a naming convention. Consistency was not cosmetic. A repeated shape: the AI optimized for a smaller artefact, and the smaller artefact kept contradicting its own claims — a cache listed in the response contract but cut from the build (3), a failure table missing one of its own rows (6). The gaps were never in what it wrote; they were in what it left out.
- **IA3** — pending.
- **IA4** — entries 9, 14, 17, 19. Entry 16 belongs here too, from the other side: a wrong value survived 27 passing tests because nothing asserted it. Green tests are evidence about what was asserted, not about what is true. Entry 9 is the one to lead with, and it extends the Part 1 answer rather than replacing it. The Part 1 answer says the failure mode is silence; entry 9 shows a second one: a specific, confident, checkable claim about a framework I had not verified. It is the second time in this exercise the AI produced a wrong quantitative or factual claim with no hedging in the output (the first was the document's word count). Both were caught by checking, neither by reading.
